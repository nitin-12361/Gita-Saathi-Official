# Privacy Policy for Gita Saathi

**Last updated:** September 18, 2026

**Gita Saathi** ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (*Gita Saathi: Bhagavad Gita AI*, package name: `com.nkapps.gitasaathi`) handles information when you use our application.

By using Gita Saathi, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

Gita Saathi is a devotional, educational, and spiritual application. We respect your privacy and do **NOT** require you to create an account, register, or provide your personal identity (such as your name, personal phone number, home address, or contacts list).

### Information Collected Automatically:
- **Device & Usage Information:** To ensure application stability, prevent crashes, and optimize audio playback, anonymous technical data (e.g., device model, OS version, app crash logs) may be collected via Google Firebase Crashlytics.
- **In-App Purchases / Subscriptions:** Subscription processing for "Gita Saathi Gold" is handled entirely and securely through the official **Google Play In-App Billing** system. We never see, collect, or store your credit/debit card details, bank account credentials, or billing addresses.
- **Spiritual AI Conversations:** When you interact with the AI spiritual guide feature, your queries are processed securely via AI APIs solely to generate contextual spiritual answers based on the Bhagavad Gita. These queries are not linked to your personal identity or sold to any third party.

## 3. Permissions Requested

Gita Saathi requests only minimal permissions necessary for its devotional features:
- **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Required to stream spiritual audio, download verses, process AI spiritual guidance, and serve ads.
- **WAKE_LOCK & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK:** Required for uninterrupted background audio playback and Sleep Timer functionality when your phone screen is turned off.
- **VIBRATE:** Used exclusively for subtle tactile feedback (haptic vibration) during the 108-bead digital Japa Mala chanting.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+):** Optional permission to deliver daily morning Shloka of the Day inspirational quotes and spiritual reminders.

We do **NOT** access your camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, or storage files.
